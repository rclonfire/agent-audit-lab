# Notification settings

Fernwheel sends email notifications so you can keep up with job activity without watching the app. This article describes each notification type and shows you how to change the address that receives them.

## Notification types

Fernwheel sends four kinds of notifications:

**Job assigned.** You get an email each time someone on your team assigns a job to you. The email includes the job name, the customer, and the scheduled time.

**Schedule changes.** You get an email when the date or time of one of your jobs changes, and when a job is canceled or rescheduled.

**Payment received.** You get an email when a customer payment posts to one of your jobs, with the amount and the job it applies to.

**Daily summary.** Once a day, Fernwheel sends a single email listing the jobs on your schedule and any changes since the last summary.

## Change your notification email

All notifications go to your notification email address. To change it:

1. Sign in to Fernwheel.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Notifications**.
4. In the **Notification email** field, enter the new address.
5. Select **Save**.

Fernwheel sends all future notifications to the new address. Emails already on their way still arrive at the old one.

## Notification email vs. login email

Your notification email can differ from the email you use to sign in. Changing the notification email does not change your login, your password, or where account security emails go. Many teams use this to route notifications to a shared inbox that a dispatcher or office manager checks, while each person keeps a personal login.

If you want to change the email you sign in with, that setting lives under **Settings > Account**, not under Notifications.

## If notifications stop arriving

1. Go to **Settings > Notifications** and confirm the notification email is spelled correctly. A typo here sends every notification to the wrong address.
2. Check the spam or junk folder in the notification inbox.
3. Add **notifications@fernwheel.example** to your contacts so your mail provider trusts future messages.
4. If emails still do not arrive after 24 hours, contact Fernwheel support at **support@fernwheel.example** and include the notification email you have set.
