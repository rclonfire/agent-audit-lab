# Fernwheel plans and pricing

Fernwheel offers three plans. Every plan is priced per technician per month, and you can change plans at any time.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Price | What it includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $29 per technician/month | Job scheduling with recurring jobs, customer SMS reminders, customer records, technician mobile app for iOS and Android |
| Crew | $59 per technician/month | Everything in Starter, plus the dispatch board, estimates and invoicing, and card payments |
| Fleet | $89 per technician/month | Everything in Crew, plus QuickBooks sync, API access, and priority support |

Admins and office staff who never get dispatched to jobs do not count as technicians and are free on every plan.

## Annual billing

You can pay monthly or annually. Annual billing gives you two months free: you pay for ten months and receive twelve. The discount applies automatically when you select annual billing at checkout or in your billing settings.

## Free trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Fleet plan, no credit card required. When the trial ends, pick a plan to keep your data and continue working. Your jobs, customers, and invoices carry over to whichever plan you choose.

## Money-back guarantee

Your first paid month carries a 30-day money-back guarantee, so if Fernwheel is not working out you can request a full refund of that first charge by emailing billing@fernwheel.example within 30 days of payment.

## Changing your plan

Plan changes are prorated. When you upgrade, the change takes effect immediately and Fernwheel adds the prorated difference for the rest of your billing cycle to your next invoice. When you downgrade, the change also takes effect immediately and the unused portion of your old plan is applied as a credit to your next invoice.

To change your plan:

1. Sign in to your account at fernwheel.example.
2. Open Settings, then select Billing.
3. Select Change plan.
4. Choose your new plan and billing period, then select Confirm change.

## Adding or removing technicians

You can add or remove technician seats at any time from Settings, then Team. Seat changes are prorated the same way plan changes are. If you have questions about your invoice, email billing@fernwheel.example.
