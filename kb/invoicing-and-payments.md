# Invoicing and Card Payments

Invoicing is available on the Crew and Fleet plans. You can turn a completed job into an invoice, send it by text or email, and accept card payments without leaving Fernwheel.

## Generate an invoice from a completed job

1. Open the job from the Jobs list or the schedule.
2. Confirm the job status is Completed. You can invoice jobs in other statuses, but completed jobs carry over labor and materials automatically.
3. Tap **Create Invoice**.
4. Review the pre-filled line items pulled from the job, then tap **Save**.

The invoice gets a number in your sequence and appears under Invoices with a Draft status until you send it.

## Add or edit line items

1. Open the invoice and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **Add Line Item**, then choose an item from your price book or enter a custom description.
3. Set the quantity and unit price. Fernwheel calculates the line total and updates the invoice total, including any tax rate you applied.
4. Tap **Save** when you finish.

You can reorder line items by dragging the handle on the left side of each row.

## Send the invoice

1. Open the invoice and tap **Send**.
2. Choose **Text** or **Email**. Fernwheel fills in the contact info from the customer record; edit it if needed.
3. Tap **Send Invoice**.

Your customer receives a secure link to view the invoice and pay by card. You get a notification when they open it and another when they pay.

## Card payments and fees

Customers can pay with any major credit or debit card through the invoice link. The processing fee is 2.9% plus 30 cents per transaction, deducted before payout. There is no monthly fee for payment processing on Crew or Fleet.

## Payout timing

Card payments arrive in your linked bank account within 2 business days of the transaction. Weekends and bank holidays do not count as business days, so a payment your customer makes on Friday typically lands Tuesday. Track pending payouts under **Settings > Payments > Payouts**.

## Mark an invoice paid manually

If a customer pays by cash or check, record it yourself so the invoice closes out.

1. Open the invoice.
2. Tap **Record Payment**.
3. Choose the payment method, enter the amount and date, and add a reference note if you want one.
4. Tap **Save**.

The invoice status changes to Paid, and the payment syncs to QuickBooks if you have the integration connected. Manual payments carry no processing fee.
