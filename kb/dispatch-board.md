# Using the dispatch board

The dispatch board gives you a live view of every job scheduled for the day and the technician assigned to each one. It is available on the Crew and Fleet plans. If you are on a lower plan, an account owner can upgrade under Settings > Billing.

## Open the board

Sign in at fernwheel.example and select Dispatch in the left sidebar. The board loads the current day by default, with one column per technician. The board updates live: when a technician changes a job status from the mobile app, the card updates on your screen within a few seconds without a refresh.

## Assign a job to a technician

Unassigned jobs appear in the holding lane on the left edge of the board.

1. Find the job in the holding lane. Select the card to preview the customer, address, and job notes.
2. Drag the card onto a technician's column, or select Assign on the card and pick a technician from the list.
3. Drop the card at the time slot you want. The job saves immediately and the technician gets a push notification on iOS or Android.

## Move a job between technicians

1. Select and hold the job card in its current column.
2. Drag it to the new technician's column and release it on the target time slot.
3. Both technicians receive an updated schedule notification. The job history records who moved it and when.

You can move a job at any point before the status reaches Complete. Moving a job that is already En Route sends the new technician the full job details, including any photos or notes the first technician added.

## Read the color states

Each card carries a color that reflects its current status:

- Gray: Scheduled. The job is assigned but the technician has not started travel.
- Blue: En Route. The technician marked themselves on the way.
- Amber: On Site. The technician checked in at the job location.
- Green: Complete. The work is done and the job is ready for invoicing through your QuickBooks sync, if connected.

A legend at the bottom of the board shows the same mapping.

## Filter by day and crew

1. Use the date picker at the top of the board to jump to any day, or use the arrow buttons to step one day at a time.
2. Select the Crew filter next to the date picker and check the crews you want to see. The board hides columns for technicians outside those crews.
3. Select Clear filters to return to the full view.

Filters apply only to your session, so each dispatcher can keep their own view.
