# Cancel or reschedule a customer appointment

You can cancel or reschedule any appointment from the Fernwheel web dashboard or the mobile app for iOS and Android. Canceling a job removes it from the technician's route for that day. Rescheduling keeps the job, its notes, and its photos, and moves it to a new time slot.

## Cancel an appointment

1. Sign in at app.fernwheel.example and open Schedule.
2. Click the appointment, then select Cancel job from the action menu.
3. Pick a cancellation reason from the list. Admins manage the list under Settings > Scheduling > Cancellation reasons.
4. Click Confirm.

On the mobile app, tap the appointment, tap the menu in the top right corner, and follow the same steps.

## Notify the customer

After you confirm, Fernwheel asks whether to notify the customer of the cancellation by text message or email. Leave the notice turned on unless the customer already knows about the change. You can edit the message before it sends, and the delivery record appears on the job's activity timeline.

## Cancellation fees

Companies that charge for late cancellations can turn on the Cancellation fees setting under Settings > Billing. When the setting is on, Fernwheel adds the fee as a line item on the customer's next invoice and syncs it to QuickBooks with the rest of your charges. The fee applies only when you pick a reason marked as billable, such as a customer no-show.

## Reschedule instead

Rescheduling keeps the job's history in one place and skips the fee question entirely.

1. Open the dispatch board.
2. Drag the appointment to a new time slot or to a different technician's column. Drag-to-reschedule works on every view except the month view.
3. Confirm the new time. Fernwheel updates the technician's route and offers to send the customer the updated details.

## Job history and reporting

Canceled jobs stay in your job history along with the reason you picked. Open Reports > Jobs and filter by status to see which reasons come up most, then adjust your booking rules or reminder timing to cut down on repeat offenders.
