# Support hours and how escalations work

Fernwheel support runs through two channels: the chat widget inside your dashboard and email at support@fernwheel.example. We do not offer phone support. Written channels keep a record of every exchange, so any team member who picks up your case can see the full history.

## Support hours

The support team is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Mountain Time. The chat assistant answers around the clock, but human specialists respond during business hours only. Messages sent outside those hours enter the queue and are handled the next business day.

## Response times

Fleet plan customers receive a first response within 4 business hours. Business hours are counted within the Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Mountain Time window. A message sent at 5:00 PM on a Friday, for example, has a first-response target of 11:00 AM the following Monday.

## What the assistant handles and what it hands off

The chat assistant resolves most routine questions on its own, including how-to guidance, job and invoice status, and plan details. Some requests always go to a person:

- All refund requests and billing disputes are escalated to a human billing specialist. The assistant will collect the details and open the case, but a specialist reviews and resolves it.
- Account closures are confirmed by a human before anything is deactivated. The assistant cannot close your account on its own.
- Anything the assistant cannot verify against your account records is handed off to the support team rather than answered with a guess.

When a handoff happens, the assistant tells you in the chat and gives you a case number.

## How to request an escalation yourself

If you want a person on your case at any point, follow these steps:

1. Type "talk to a person" in the chat widget, or reply to any support email asking for a specialist.
2. Include your account email and, if you have one, the case number from an earlier conversation.
3. Describe what you have already tried. This lets the specialist skip steps you have covered.
4. Watch your email for the response. Escalated cases follow the same 4 business hour first-response target during support hours.

Escalated cases stay in the same thread, so you never need to restart the conversation or repeat your history.
