# Closing your Fernwheel account

You can close your Fernwheel account at any time from the web app. This article covers exporting your data, submitting the closure request, and what happens to your billing and data afterward.

## Export your data first

Once closure is confirmed, you lose access to your account, so download anything you want to keep before you submit the request.

1. Sign in at fernwheel.example.
2. Go to Settings > Data.
3. Select Export my data.
4. Choose the records you want and select Download. Fernwheel packages your records as CSV files and emails a download link to your account address.

Large exports can take up to an hour to arrive. Wait for the export email before you continue.

## Close your account

1. Go to Settings > Account.
2. Select Close account.
3. Enter your password to confirm your identity.
4. Share a reason for leaving if you like, then select Submit closure request.

A person on the Fernwheel support team reviews every closure request. You will receive a confirmation email from support@fernwheel.example within two business days. Your account stays open until that confirmation arrives, so watch your inbox and reply to the email if anything looks wrong.

## Billing after closure

Billing stops at the end of your current billing period. You keep full access until that date, and Fernwheel issues no charges after it. Closing your account does not trigger a refund for time already billed; your subscription runs through the period end and then terminates.

## Data retention and deletion

Fernwheel retains your data for 90 days after closure. During that window you can reopen your account and recover everything by emailing support@fernwheel.example from your account address. After 90 days, Fernwheel permanently deletes your records from its systems, including backups, and no one can recover them. If you skipped the export step, request a final data export within the 90-day window.

## Need help

Email support@fernwheel.example with the subject line "Account closure" if you run into trouble at any step, or if you submitted a closure request by mistake and want to keep your account open. A support teammate will respond within one business day.
