# Getting started with Fernwheel

Fernwheel runs the daily work of a trades business: scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, and the office view behind it all. This article walks you from signup to your first scheduled job.

## Create your account

1. Go to fernwheel.example and select **Start free trial**.
2. Enter your name, work email, and a password. The trial runs 14 days and does not require a card.
3. Open the confirmation email and select the verification link.
4. Sign in at app.fernwheel.example.

## Set up your company profile

Your company profile appears on estimates, invoices, and customer notifications, so complete it before you send anything.

1. Select the gear icon in the lower left sidebar to open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Settings > Company Profile**.
3. Enter your business name, phone number, service address, and logo.
4. Set your time zone and default business hours. Fernwheel uses these when it offers appointment windows.
5. Select **Save**.

If you use QuickBooks for accounting, you can connect it later under **Settings > Integrations**. It is not required to finish setup.

## Add your first technician

1. Go to **Settings > Team** and select **Add team member**.
2. Enter the technician's name and email address, then choose the **Technician** role.
3. Select **Send invite**. The technician gets an email with a link to install the Fernwheel app for iOS or Android.

Once the technician signs in on their phone, they appear on the dispatch board and can receive jobs.

## Schedule your first job

1. Select **Schedule** in the sidebar.
2. Select **New Job**, then add the customer's name, address, and contact details. New customers are saved automatically.
3. Describe the work and pick a job type, such as service call or install.
4. Choose a date, an arrival window, and the technician.
5. Select **Create Job**. The technician sees it in their app, and the customer receives a confirmation message if notifications are turned on.

## Invite your office staff

Office staff work from the desktop app, where they can see the full schedule, answer customers, and send invoices.

1. Go to **Settings > Team** and select **Add team member**.
2. Choose the **Office** role and enter the person's email.
3. Select **Send invite**. They set their own password from the invite email.

## Next steps

From here, set up your price book under **Settings > Price Book**, then send your first invoice from a completed job. If you get stuck, contact support@fernwheel.example.
