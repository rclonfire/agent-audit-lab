# Adding and removing technicians

## How seats work

Fernwheel bills per technician seat. Each active technician on your account counts as one seat on your monthly invoice. Office users who only dispatch or handle billing do not use a technician seat.

If you add a technician partway through a billing cycle, Fernwheel prorates the charge for the remaining days and adds it to your next invoice. If you remove a technician mid-cycle, Fernwheel credits the unused days on your next invoice. You do not need to time changes to your billing date.

## Add a technician

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Click **Add technician**.
3. Enter the technician's name and email address.
4. Choose their role and click **Send invite**.

The technician receives an email invite at the address you entered. After they accept the invite and set a password, they can sign in on the web or through the Fernwheel app on iOS or Android. Billing for the seat starts on the day you send the invite.

## Resend an invite

Invites expire after 7 days. If a technician never received the email or let the link expire:

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Find the technician listed with a **Pending** status.
3. Open the menu next to their name and choose **Resend invite**.

The new link replaces the old one, so any earlier invite email will no longer work.

## Deactivate or delete a technician

Deactivate a technician when they leave your company or take extended time off. A deactivated technician can no longer sign in, and their seat comes off your bill, but their job history, notes, photos, and time entries stay on the account. You can reactivate them later from the same page.

Delete a technician only when you need the account gone permanently, such as a duplicate created by mistake. Completed jobs stay on record for reporting, but the deleted account cannot be restored.

To deactivate or delete:

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Click the technician's name.
3. Choose **Deactivate** or **Delete**, then confirm.

Either action stops billing for the seat and posts a prorated credit on your next invoice. If you are unsure which to pick, choose **Deactivate**. You can always delete the account later.
