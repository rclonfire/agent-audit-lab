# Billing, invoices, and refunds

## When invoices generate

Fernwheel generates monthly invoices on the 1st of each month; annual invoices generate on your renewal date. You receive a copy at the billing email on your account, and every invoice is stored under Settings > Billing > Invoices in your dashboard at app.fernwheel.example.

## Understanding your invoice ID

Every invoice carries an ID in the format INV-1042. If your account was migrated from our earlier billing system, imported invoices carry a letter suffix, for example INV-1042A. The suffix marks the invoice as a legacy import and changes nothing about the amount, due date, or payment terms. Include the full ID, suffix and all, whenever you contact us about a specific invoice.

## Card processing fees

Payments made by card include a processing fee of 2.9% plus 30 cents per transaction. The fee appears as a separate line item on your invoice so you can see the subscription charge and the processing cost side by side. Annual plans paid by card carry the same rate.

## How to update your card

1. Sign in at app.fernwheel.example.
2. Open Settings > Billing.
3. Select Payment method, then Update card.
4. Enter the new card number, expiration date, and security code.
5. Select Save. Fernwheel runs a small authorization check and releases it within a few days.

The new card takes effect on your next invoice. If a payment already failed this cycle, we retry the new card within 24 hours of the update, so you do not need to contact support to trigger a retry.

## Refunds

Refunds on monthly charges are available within 14 days of the charge date. To request one, reply to the invoice email or open a support conversation and include the invoice ID and a short note on the reason.

A billing specialist reviews and processes every refund request; the assistant cannot issue refunds. Once a specialist approves your request, the refund returns to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your card issuer. The card processing fee from the original transaction is refunded along with the charge.

If a charge looks wrong but falls outside the refund window, contact support anyway. A specialist can review the invoice for billing errors, which are corrected regardless of when they occurred.
